Everything your job hunt needs

From first application to accepted offer, GigMaster gives you full visibility over your pipeline.

Application Pipeline

Log every application with company, role, platform, and recruiting agent. Track status from Applied through to Offer or Rejected — nothing gets lost.

Interview Scheduler

Attach full interview details to any application — date, time, interviewer, meeting link, documents required, and notes. All tied to the right opportunity.

Dual-Profile Tracking

Manage job hunts for two people simultaneously. Each profile has its own filtered view so you always stay focused on what matters.

Rich Status System

7 application statuses (Applied, Screening, Interviewing, Offer, Rejected, Withdrawn, Ghosted) and 5 interview states keep your pipeline crystal clear.

Follow-Up Dates

Set a follow-up date on any application so you always know when to chase. The calendar view shows everything coming up at a glance.

Source Tracking

Record where every application came from — LinkedIn, Indeed, direct, referral. Know which channels are actually converting to interviews.
